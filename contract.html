Project Intake Checklist

This checklist helps you prepare for a written project discussion. It is not a live submission form, contract, or request for credentials.

Helpful Information to Prepare

  • Business name, existing website, and public contact details.
  • Services offered, service area, and typical customers.
  • Pages you need and the main action visitors should take.
  • Existing logo, approved photos, brand colors, and written content.
  • Any contact, quote, booking, or integration requirements.
  • Preferred timeline and a working budget range, if known.
  • Existing domain, hosting, or third-party tools that may be relevant.

Keep Access Secure

Please do not send passwords, payment-card data, private customer records, or calendar credentials in a message. If access is needed for an agreed project, use a secure invitation or permission method supported by the relevant service.

What Happens Next?

We review the information, discuss any missing details in writing, and agree on deliverables, pricing, timeline, revisions, third-party expenses, and handoff before paid work starts.
