THE MAISON EMBER APPROACH

Made for the moments that bring us together.

Maison Ember is an imagined neighborhood restaurant inspired by the warmth of open-fire cooking and the generous spirit of Mediterranean dining. The concept pairs thoughtful seasonal plates with an intimate, contemporary atmosphere.

Every detail—from the first welcome to the final course—is designed around good food and unhurried conversation.
