RESOURCES / PROJECT PLANNING

Start with a clear plan.

Use these checklists to organize your ideas before requesting a project quote. They are practical planning aids, not guarantees of business results.

01. Small-business website

  • Clear description of services and target customers.
  • Business name, service area, hours, and written contact details.
  • One clear action: contact, request a quote, or book.
  • Mobile-friendly pages, readable headings, and descriptive image text.
  • Privacy policy and terms that reflect the actual services and tools used.

02. Contact & quote form

  • Ask only for information needed to respond.
  • Label every field and explain which fields are required.
  • Confirm the delivery destination and response process.
  • Set a clear success message or thank-you page.
  • Test the form with permission before advertising it.

03. Booking setup

  • List bookable services, durations, hours, and buffer times.
  • Confirm time zones, cancellation rules, reminders, and calendar access.
  • Review recurring subscription and messaging fees.
  • Decide who maintains availability and handles booking problems.

04. Digital workflow planning

  • Name the repetitive task and how often it happens.
  • Record the trigger, inputs, expected result, and responsible person.
  • Plan what happens if information is missing or the tool fails.
  • Review account access, customer data, and retention needs.
  • Compare setup costs with recurring tools, usage, and maintenance costs.
