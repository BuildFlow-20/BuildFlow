HELP / COMMON QUESTIONS

Good questions deserve clear answers.

A few useful details about how projects work, what we offer, and how to get started.

GENERAL & PROJECT QUESTIONS
What does BuildFlow Support do?

We focus on small-business websites, website improvements, contact and quote forms, booking flows, and carefully scoped digital workflow projects.

How do I start a project?

Send a message through the contact form or email Support@buildflowsupport.org. Describe your business, goal, and any features you have in mind.

Do you communicate by phone?

Project communication is handled through email and written messages. We do not require a phone call to begin an inquiry.

How much does a website cost?

Starting estimates are listed on the Pricing page. The final quote depends on page count, content, functionality, integrations, and project requirements.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on scope, content readiness, feedback, and any third-party tools. We agree on a realistic schedule before work begins.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes, subject to access to the current site and a review of its platform, code, and requirements. We confirm what can be changed before quoting.

Are the portfolio examples paid client projects?

No. The items identified as demonstrations are sample concepts and are not presented as paid client projects or verified client results.

Are third-party subscriptions included?

Not unless a written quote specifically says so. Hosting, domain registration, booking tools, CRM subscriptions, messaging fees, and other external charges may be separate.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Ongoing maintenance or support can be discussed as a separate, clearly defined scope. It is not automatically included in a one-time setup.

Still have a question?

Send it in writing and we will review it.
