ACCESSIBILITY / OUR COMMITMENT

A clearer experience for more people.

We aim to make this website clear, usable, and welcoming to people using assistive technology or who prefer written communication.

01 / STRUCTURE

Clear structure

Meaningful page titles, headings, landmarks, and descriptive links help people understand and navigate content.

02 / KEYBOARD

Keyboard access

Interactive elements should be reachable and usable with a keyboard, with visible focus indicators.

03 / READABILITY

Readable content

We aim for legible text, sufficient contrast, simple instructions, and layouts that work on different screen sizes.

04 / FORMS

Accessible forms

Forms should have associated labels, clear required-field instructions, and understandable feedback.

Found an accessibility barrier?

Tell us which page you were using and what made it difficult. We will review the report and consider a practical improvement.
