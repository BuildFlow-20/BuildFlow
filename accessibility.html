Accessibility at BuildFlow Support

We aim to make our website clear, usable, and welcoming to as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology or prefer written communication.

Our Accessibility Approach

Clear Structure

Use meaningful page titles, headings, landmarks, and descriptive links to help people understand and navigate content.

Keyboard Access

Interactive elements should be reachable and usable with a keyboard, with visible focus indicators.

Readable Content

We aim for legible text, sufficient contrast, simple instructions, and layouts that work on different screen sizes.

Accessible Forms

Forms should have associated labels, clear required-field instructions, and understandable feedback.

Need an Accessibility Improvement?

If you find a barrier on this website or need information in a different format, contact us by email or the written contact form. Tell us which page you were using and what made it difficult. We will review the report and consider a practical improvement.

Report an Accessibility Issue

This statement describes our approach; it is not a certification or a guarantee that every page or third-party service meets every accessibility standard. Accessibility is an ongoing process and third-party tools may have their own limitations.
