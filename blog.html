FIELD NOTES / PRACTICAL GUIDES

Ideas for a stronger digital presence.

Short, useful guidance for small businesses planning a website, improving customer inquiries, or considering digital tools.

LATEST GUIDES

01. Make your homepage answer the basics

Visitors should quickly understand what you do, who you serve, and what to do next. Use plain language, a clear service area, useful business details, and one primary action.

  • Describe your main service in the first screen.
  • Show location or service area when relevant.
  • Make the next step obvious, such as requesting a quote.

02. Make contact simple and accessible

A contact form should ask only for information needed to respond. Use visible labels, clear instructions, keyboard-friendly controls, and an understandable confirmation message. Test where submissions are delivered before promoting the form.

03. Choose booking tools carefully

Before adding an appointment scheduler, check time zones, availability rules, cancellation policies, notifications, and any subscription or transaction fees. Make sure the business owner can maintain the schedule.

04. Plan automation before buying software

Start with the repetitive task, not the tool. Map what triggers the task, what information is involved, what should happen next, and when a person needs to review an exception.

05. Check mobile and keyboard use

Review pages on a phone and navigate with a keyboard. Use descriptive headings, readable text, visible focus states, labelled form fields, and meaningful link text.

06. Agree on project scope before work begins

List the pages, features, content responsibilities, revision limits, timeline, and handoff. Confirm who pays for hosting, domains, email, booking tools, and subscriptions.
